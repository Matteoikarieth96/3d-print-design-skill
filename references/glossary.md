# Glossary for a first print

One line each, to reuse in questions, the print sheet and the hand-off. Use
the person's language; keep the English term in brackets the first time,
because the slicer shows it in English.

- **Nozzle**: the hot tip the melted plastic comes out of; 0.4 mm on most printers. Walls thinner than 2 nozzles (0.8 mm) do not print well.
- **Layer height**: how thick each printed layer is; 0.2 mm is the usual. Thinner looks smoother and takes longer.
- **Walls (wall loops, perimeters)**: the lines that make the outer shell. They give most of the strength: 2 for looks, 3 for everyday parts, 4 for loads and screws.
- **Infill**: the pattern that fills the inside, as a percentage. 10 to 15 % for most parts, 40 % for heavy loads; 100 % is rarely worth the time.
- **Top and bottom layers**: the solid layers that close the part above and below the infill.
- **Overhang**: a part of the model hanging in the air. Up to about 45 degrees from vertical prints fine; more needs supports or a design change.
- **Supports**: throw-away scaffolding the slicer adds under overhangs; it leaves marks and takes time. Good designs avoid them.
- **Brim**: a thin ring around the first layer that helps tall or narrow parts stay stuck to the plate.
- **Bed / build plate**: the flat plate the part is printed on. The face that touches it is the flattest one.
- **Elephant foot**: the first layers squash a little wider; a small chamfer on the bottom edge hides it.
- **PLA / PETG / TPU / ABS-ASA**: PLA is the easiest and stiffest, softens in a hot car; PETG is tougher and takes more heat; TPU is rubbery; ABS and ASA take heat and sun but need ventilation.
- **Filament**: the plastic wire on the spool, 1.75 mm thick; 1 m of PLA weighs about 3 g.
- **Slicer**: the program (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura) that turns the 3D model into printer instructions and gives the exact time and grams.
- **STL / 3MF**: STL is the shape only; a 3MF project also holds the colours and settings.
- **Purge**: filament thrown away at each colour change on the same nozzle, so the new colour comes out clean.
- **AMS / external spool**: on Bambu printers the AMS is the box that holds 4 spools and changes them automatically; the external spool hangs on the back. On the X2D the AMS feeds the left nozzle, the external spool the right one.
