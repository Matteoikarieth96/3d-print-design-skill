# Example: calibration coupon

The tolerance and overhang coupon generated by `scripts/calibration_coupon.py`
with its default settings (6 mm pegs). Nothing here is tied to a real printer;
the `fits.json` values in `templates/fits.example.json` are fictional.

![coupon](../../docs/calibration-coupon.png)

- Plate with 9 round and 9 square holes at 0.00 to 0.40 mm clearance per side
  in 0.05 mm steps, identified by V notches (shallow = 1 step, deep = 5).
- Two round pegs and one square bar, chamfered at both ends.
- Five overhang fins at 30, 40, 45, 50 and 60 degrees, front to back.

Rebuild from this folder: `sh build.sh` (uses the repo's `.venv` if present,
else `python3`). How to read and record the results:
[references/calibration.md](../../references/calibration.md).
